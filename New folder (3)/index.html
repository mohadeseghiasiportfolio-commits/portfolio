About Me

I’m a visual artist and graphic design student, and I like exploring different ways of working with images. My work moves between photography, painting, illustration and graphic design, depending on the idea and what I want to say.

I’m mostly interested in visual storytelling, composition and atmosphere. I’m still learning and experimenting, and I enjoy letting each project lead me toward a different visual language.
